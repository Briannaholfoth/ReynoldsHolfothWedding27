This Isn't Just a Wedding. It's a Trip.

We're getting married between Naples and the Almalfi Coast of Italy, and we want you there for the whole experience, not just one afternoon. Think of this as your invitation to a three-day getaway: an optional day trip to Capri, the wedding itself, and a lazy morning brunch to send everyone off. Why Sorrento

Prego, and Welcome

We fell for Italy for a hundred small reasons and a few big ones: the history, the food, the wine, the way everyone says "prego" like it solves everything. We bounced around a handful of cities before Sorrento won us over completely, the coast, the water, the pace of it.

Now we get to share it with the people we love most.
