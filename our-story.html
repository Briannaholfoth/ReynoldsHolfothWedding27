Our Story

It Started at the Pool

Brandon spotted the loud, funny girl surrounded by friends and thought, who is that? Bri spotted the quiet guy with the blue eyes (and, let's be honest, the calves) and thought, he seems cool.

That should have been the whole story right there. Instead, it took two years.

Two years of being friends first. Two years of near misses and bad timing, the kind where you both know something's there but neither of you says it out loud. And through it all, the same crew of friends around the pool most weekends, the people who watched this whole thing unfold before we said a word about it.

And then, on one summer evening that neither of us saw coming and both of us had been waiting for, we finally kissed. That was it. We haven't looked back since.

Almost four years later, on a cliff overlooking the coast of New Zealand, Brandon got down on one knee. Nobody was around to catch it on camera, so all we've got is the view, which honestly feels about right.

In between: ten countries together, two very spoiled dogs, a lot of gardening, a lot of concerts (we're both music people, no question), Sunday yoga classes Brandon now willingly attends, hikes, bike rides, and a shared, borderline-competitive love of finding good food wherever we are.

Why Italy

Because It Gets You

Italy has a way of getting under your skin. The food, the wine, the fact that you can be standing in front of something that existed before Christ and just... feel small in the best way.

Brandon is a history person through and through. Bri is obsessed with cultures and languages. Italy is the one place that hands you both at once, on every single street corner.

We've said "prego" more times than we can count, ordered a hundred drinks just for the free snacks that come with them, and fallen in love with a good limoncello. We bounced around a handful of Italian cities before landing here. Florence came close, but Sorrento won, the coast, the water, the whole slower, sun-warmed pace of it. It felt like the only place that made sense to bring the people we love most.
