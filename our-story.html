Our Story

It Started at the Pool

Brandon spotted the loud, funny girl surrounded by friends and thought, who is that? Bri spotted the quiet guy with the blue eyes (and, let's be honest, the calves) and thought, he seems cool.

That should have been the whole story right there. Instead, it took two years.

Two years of being friends first. Two years of laughter, fun, and buildup. The kind where you both know something deep is there but neither of you says it out loud. Is it friendship or is it something more? And through it all, the same crew of friends around the pool most weekends, the people who watched this whole thing unfold before we said a word about it.

And then, on one summer evening that neither of us saw coming, we finally kissed. That was it. We haven't looked back since.

After 4 years, Brandon decided it was time to "go out to pasture and hang his cowboy hat up." They had a trip to New Zealand coming up, but Brandon was afraid he would lose the hardware. So, he made Bri climb the highest mountain in Bloomington (aka Hyland Hills if you're familiar) to ask for her hand in marriage. He said it would be a practice run and he would ask again in New Zealand, which he did on a cliff overlooking the coast while they were on a hike with Bri's family.

Everything in between: spoiling two amazing dogs (don't bring up the most popular dog breed in front of us unless you're up for debating), a lot of gardening, tons of concerts (music is a love language we both share), Sunday yoga classes Brandon now willingly attends and hates to miss! Hikes, bike rides, and a shared, borderline-competitive love of finding good food wherever we are. Their love of sports deserves an entire segment, but for time sake: Brandon was adopted into the Bills Mafia and Bri accepts that screaming skol will never end well. It's hard loving teams that just can't get it right, but Go Bills!

Why Italy

Because It Gets You

Italy has a way of getting under your skin. The food, the wine, the fact that you can be standing in front of something that existed before Christ and just... feel small in the best way.

Brandon is a history person through and through. Bri is obsessed with cultures and languages. Italy is the one place that hands you both at once, on every single street corner.

We've said "prego" more times than we can count, ordered a hundred drinks just for the free snacks that come with them, and fallen in love with a good limoncello. We bounced around a handful of Italian cities before landing here. Florence came close, but Sorrento won, the coast, the water, the whole slower, sun-warmed pace of it. It felt like the only place that made sense to bring the people we love most.
