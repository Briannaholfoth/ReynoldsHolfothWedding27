Good to Know

FAQ

What should I expect from this trip?

Let's be real: this isn't a resort-and-done kind of trip. There's a long flight, train travel, a currency and language that aren't your own (though most Italians speak English), and a few logistics to sort out. It takes a little effort. But that effort is kind of the whole point, it's what makes it a real trip instead of a vacation package. Come ready for an adventure, not a walk in the park.

How should I prepare?

Get your body ready as much as your suitcase, there's a lot of walking, hills, and stairs. Hiking and general mobility work in the months before helps more than you'd think. Also hit your bank before you leave and convert some cash to euros, most of Europe runs on card, but you'll want cash on hand for tips (drivers, tour guides, the occasional Uber). For a week, €200 is plenty. For 8–10 days, bring €300–400.

Will I need a visa?

No visa, but you will need an ETIAS travel authorization, a quick, inexpensive online screening the EU is rolling out for visa-exempt countries. It's expected to be required by the time of the wedding. We'll update this page with the application link once it's live.

What's the weather like?

Highs around 74°F, lows around 63°F. Layers recommended. Earlier in October tends to be nicer than later in the month.

Are kids invited?

We recommend leaving little ones at home if it means you can still make it, but if that's the deciding factor, talk to us, we want you there above all else.

Can I bring a plus one?

Ask Bri or Brandon directly, it'll most likely be a yes.

Do I need to attend all three days?

No, the Capri day and Friday brunch are both optional. Thursday's the wedding, that's the one day we'd love everyone there for.

What if I can't make the Capri ferry day?

No problem, it's optional, just check in Wednesday evening instead.

Where should I stay?

See the Where to Stay page, options range from budget-friendly on Via Nastro Verde to walkable options in town near Piazza Tasso.

Is there a wedding hotel shuttle?

Yes, see the Where to Stay page for the full schedule.

No gifts, please. Your being here is the gift. Read more on our No Gifts page.
