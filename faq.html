Good to Know

FAQ

What should I expect from this trip?

Let's be real: this isn't a resort-and-done kind of trip. There's a long flight, train travel, a currency and language that aren't your own (though most Italians speak English), and a few logistics to sort out. It takes a little effort. But that effort makes the trip all the more while. Come ready for an picturesqu adventure.

How should I prepare?

Get your body ready as much as your suitcase, there's a lot of walking, hills, and stairs. Hiking and general mobility work in the months before helps more than you'd think. Brandon's advice: "Put some bowling balls in your suitcase and practice carting it up and down stairs." The hills there aren't a joke.

Will I need a visa?

No visa, but you will need an ETIAS travel authorization, a quick, inexpensive online screening the EU is rolling out for visa-exempt countries. It's expected to be required by the time of the wedding. We'll update this page with the application link once it's live.

What's the weather like?

Highs around 74°F, lows around 63°F. Layers recommended. Earlier in October tends to be nicer than later in the month.

Are kids invited?

We recommend leaving little ones at home if it means you can still make it, but if that's the deciding factor, talk to us, we want you there above all else.

Can I bring a plus one?

Ask Bri or Brandon directly, it'll most likely be a yes.

Any tips on language, money, and culture?

Number 1: take some time to research the culture and where you want to go, it's fun! Download Google Translate. You can read menu's/sign's better and have a full conversation with someone who doesn't speak English. Hit your bank 3 weeks before you leave and convert some cash to euros, most of Europe runs on card, but you'll want cash on hand for tips (drivers, tour guides, the occasional Uber). For a week, €200 is plenty. For 8–10 days, bring €300–400. Europe isn't a tipping culture, but America is. We stick out like sore thumbs, so sometimes they do expect it. Also, if you receive excellent service and you feel it's appropriate, then tip.

Number 2: Learn some basic Italian phrases. Plenty of free language apps, or just keep a cheat sheet of the basic ones to review before you go out.

Number 3: Get comfortable walking shoes. Being stylish is sooo Italian, extra points for that!

Do I need to attend all three days?

No, the Capri day and Friday brunch are both optional. Thursday's the wedding, that's the one day we'd love everyone there for. Italy has a lot to offer, please enjoy the vacation YOU want.

What if I can't make the Capri ferry day?

No problem, it's optional, just check in Wednesday evening instead.
