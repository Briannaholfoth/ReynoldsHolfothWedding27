Good to Know

Attire & Packing

Dress Code

Cocktail to formal. Whatever makes you feel good! The ceremony will be outside weather permitting. Dinner and reception indoors.

Weather

Expect highs around 74°F and lows around 63°F. Plan on layers for cooler evenings.

Don't Forget!

  • Passport (check the expiration date now, most countries require 6+ months validity beyond your travel dates). Email a copy to yourself and travel partner. Print a copy out if you can too.
  • Good walking shoes, you'll be on cobblestone and hills more than you expect
  • Something dressier for the wedding, more casual/comfortable for the Capri day and brunch
  • A light jacket or sweater for evenings
  • Sunscreen and a hat, October sun in Italy is still real sun
  • A day bag for the Capri ferry day
  • A portable phone charger. You will be using google maps to walk around and taking videos/photos. Your phone will die, so having a backup gives you peace of mind.
  • A plug adapter (Italy uses Type C/F/L outlets)
  • Motion sickness meds if you're not used to ferries, just in case
  • Cash and Coins, for tipping drivers and smaller shops that don't take card. Also, you need coins to pay to use bathrooms in some cases. Get Euros from your bank a month before departure.
  • A cross-body or anti-theft bag, keeps your money, cards, and passport close in busy tourist areas
  • Medications and anything you'll need to assist with sleep, pain, etc.

Phone, Connectivity, and Community WhatsApp Page

Call your carrier before you leave and ask about international plans, or check that your phone is unlocked if you'd rather use an eSIM once you land, usually the cheaper and easier route. Either way, sort this out before you go, not at the airport. More money-saving prep tips like this are on Bri's Booking Hacks.

  • You'll need to download WhatsApp. It's free calling anywhere in Europe, so it's for safety, but also communicating with your group and the future wedding friends you meet.
  • After you've RSVP'd you'll be added to a Group What'sApp page for community updates if you select to. This is a place where people can ask questions, get updates, post great food finds, etc. As always, you can silence this and check in on your terms.
  • Whether you utilize your carrier or purchase an eSim, make sure you have enough data or can easily add on to avoid future stress.
