Good to Know

Attire & Packing

Dress Code

Cocktail or beach formal.

Weather

Expect highs around 74°F and lows around 63°F. Plan on layers for cooler evenings.

Don't Forget

  • Passport (check the expiration date now, most countries require 6+ months validity beyond your travel dates)
  • Good walking shoes, you'll be on cobblestone and hills more than you expect
  • Something dressier for the wedding, more casual/comfortable for the Capri day and brunch
  • A light jacket or sweater for evenings
  • Sunscreen and a hat, October sun in Italy is still real sun
  • A day bag for the Capri ferry day
  • A portable phone charger. You will be using google maps to walk around and taking videos/photos. Your phone will die, so having a backup gives you peace of mind.
  • A plug adapter (Italy uses Type C/F/L outlets)
  • Motion sickness meds if you're not used to ferries, just in case
  • Cash, for tipping drivers and smaller shops that don't take card. Also, you need to pay to use bathrooms in some cases.
  • A cross-body or anti-theft bag, keeps your money, cards, and passport close in busy tourist areas
  • A photo of your passport and cards saved to your phone, plus a physical photocopy packed separately from the originals

Phone & Connectivity

Call your carrier before you leave and ask about international plans, or check that your phone is unlocked if you'd rather use an eSIM once you land, usually the cheaper and easier route. Either way, sort this out before you go, not at the airport. More money-saving prep tips like this are on Bri's Booking Hacks.
