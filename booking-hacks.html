Bri's Booking Hacks

Italy sounds expensive until you actually price it out. It doesn't have to break the bank, and we want you there, so here's exactly how we'd book this trip if we were you.

Ballpark budget: flight + hotel bundled together can run somewhere around $1,500–2,000 per person, sometimes less depending on when you book. For two people, that's often under $3,000 total for both flights and lodging, before food and activities.

How We'd Book It: Delta Vacations

We've used Delta Vacations for our own trips and keep coming back to it. You bundle your flight and hotel into a single package and a single payment, instead of juggling two separate bookings and two separate bills.

The part we really like: you put down a deposit to lock in the package, then chip away at the rest with a payment plan, say $300 or $400 a month, however often works for you, so that by the time you actually travel, most of the trip is already paid off. All that's left to spend once you land is food and whatever you want to buy while you're there.

Pay it off a couple months early and you can start setting aside a separate spending budget instead, so you land with the trip covered and cash ready to go, not scrambling to catch up when you get home.

Travel insurance: you can add it right through Delta Vacations when you book. One nice perk, it lets you cancel up to 30 days before takeoff. Since you're booking this far in advance, that's a lot of runway for peace of mind if plans change.

Money-Saving Flight Tips

  • Fly Saturday to Saturday. This tends to be the cheapest window for international fares.
  • Fly into Rome. Generally the more affordable entry point compared to flying directly into Naples.
  • Book early, but watch for price drops. Airline and bundle prices shift, it's worth checking back periodically rather than assuming the first price you see is the final one.

Before You Go

  • Call your cell phone carrier. Ask about international plans, or make sure your phone is unlocked if you want to use an eSIM instead, it's usually cheaper and easier than paying for a full international plan.
  • Check your passport now. Make sure it's current and won't expire within 6 months of your travel dates.
  • Get a cross-body or anti-theft bag. Something that keeps your money, cards, and passport close to your body. Pickpocketing happens in busy tourist areas, a secure bag is a small thing that saves a lot of stress.
  • Back up your passport and credit cards. Take a photo of both sides of each and email it to yourself, plus keep a physical photocopy in a separate bag from the originals. If anything gets lost or stolen, you'll still have what you need to get money and get home.
