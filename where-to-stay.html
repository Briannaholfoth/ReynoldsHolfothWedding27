The Wedding Hotel

The wedding venue and morning brunch are at the Grand Hotel President, ghpresident.com This hotel has a spa with services. Includes: a sauna, turkish bath, and hot tub. It also has a game room, where the after party will be held.

Room block reservations open January 2027, with a 4-night minimum stay. Rooms start at $440+/night for a garden view room at that time frame in October and go up from there. We have secured a discounted Sea View Room for guests at 400 Euros a night with breakfast included. We'll share the direct booking link the moment it's available.

Pick Your Tradeoff

There isn't one right answer here, it really comes down to what matters most to you: price, quiet, view, or convenience.

  • Near or on Via Nastro Verde (the street of the hotel): budget-friendly options nearby, quiet, mountainside, but uphill the entire way. You'll rely on the hotel shuttle, a rented car or VESPA to get to/from town.
  • In town, near Piazza Tasso: Typically more affordable or even cheaper but can be louder. This is the easiest option for transfers since it's central to everything and near the shuttle drop point.
  • On the coast: the priciest tier, quieter than town, and the views are unbeatable. However, you can find good prices between the hotel and town for the best of both worlds.
Worth knowing: The Via Nastro Verde street is entirely uphill. (To provide the best views, IMO) The President (and Grand Aminta) run a complimentary shuttle to and from Sorrento center, guests just need to mention they're with the hotel or wedding.

Shuttle to Sorrento center: 8:30, 9:30, 10:30, 11:30, 12:30, 15:30, 16:30, 17:30, 18:30, 19:30, 20:30, 21:30, 22:30
Shuttle back to hotel: 8:50, 9:50, 10:50, 11:50, 12:50, 15:50, 16:50, 17:50, 18:50, 19:50, 20:50, 21:50, 22:50

Pickup in town is via degli Aranci, near Hotel Cesare Augusto, close to Piazza Tasso. If you're staying in town, it's about a 20-minute walk downhill from the venue after the wedding, and an easy way to close out the night.

Our Personal Pick

Grand Hotel Aminta — we stayed here before and it was amazing! It's over $700 cheaper for the four nights than the Grand President. The food, incredible. The views, jaw-dropping. The service, fantastic. Antonio is one of the bartenders that we learned a lot from. Of course, we'd love you at our hotel, but it's a 8 minute walk away and another wonderful option. We would've gotten married here, but they didn't have a coordinator. They also share a shuttle with our hotel. aminta.com

Other Affordable Options

  • AirBNB or VRBO — B&B, Book these early since many Europeans holiday in southern Italy. There are some gorgeous ones higher up on the hill that look beautiful.
  • Atlantic Palace Hotel — 4-star, garden setting, roughly $150–235/night.
  • Johanna Park Hotel — 4-star, beautiful interior, shuttle to town, roughly $170–265/night.
  • Villa Giovanna — well-reviewed B&B, roughly $160–280/night depending on room and season. Rooms that sleep 4 and offers babysitting.
  • Eden Villa Sorrento — B&B, budget-friendly.
  • Villa Ada — B&B, This is a Bed and Breakfast or can be rented out through the Grand Hotel President like a cabin.
  • Hotels in town — B&B, there are plenty of affordable hotels in the heart of Sorrento

Generally, expect this range of options to fall between $20–250+/night.
