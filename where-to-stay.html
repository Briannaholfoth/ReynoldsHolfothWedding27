Where to Stay

The Wedding Hotel

The wedding venue is at the Grand Hotel President, and it's the heart of the weekend. ghpresident.com

Room block reservations open November 2026, with a 3-night minimum stay. Rooms start at $230/night and go up from there. We'll share the direct booking link the moment it's available.

Pick Your Tradeoff

There isn't one right answer here, it really comes down to what matters most to you: price, quiet, or convenience.

  • Near or on Via Nastro Verde (the street of the hotel): budget-friendly options nearby, quiet, mountainside, but uphill the entire way. You'll rely on the hotel shuttle, a rented car or VESPA to get to/from town.
  • In town, near Piazza Tasso: Can be just as affordable or even cheaper but louder. This is the easiest option for transfers since it's central to everything and near the shuttle drop point.
  • On the coast: the priciest tier, quieter than town, and the views are unbeatable.
Worth knowing: The Via Nastro Verde street is entirely uphill. (To provide the best views, IMO) The President (and Grand Aminta) run a complimentary shuttle to and from Sorrento center, guests just need to mention they're with the wedding.

Shuttle to Sorrento center: 8:30, 9:30, 10:30, 11:30, 12:30, 15:30, 16:30, 17:30, 18:30, 19:30, 20:30, 21:30, 22:30
Shuttle back to hotel: 8:50, 9:50, 10:50, 11:50, 12:50, 15:50, 16:50, 17:50, 18:50, 19:50, 20:50, 21:50, 22:50

Pickup in town is via degli Aranci, near Hotel Cesare Augusto, close to Piazza Tasso. If you're staying in town, it's about a 20-minute walk downhill from the venue after the wedding, and an easy way to close out the night.

Our Personal Pick

Grand Hotel Aminta — we stayed here and it was amazing. The food, incredible. The views, jaw-dropping. The service, fantastic. Antonio is one of the bartenders that we learned a lot from. aminta.com

Other Affordable Options

  • Johanna Park Hotel — 4-star, garden setting, roughly $150–235/night.
  • Villa Giovanna — well-reviewed B&B, roughly $95–450/night depending on room and season.
  • Eden Villa Sorrento — B&B, budget-friendly.
  • Villa Ada — B&B, budget-friendly.

Generally, expect this range of options to fall between $130–250+/night.
