Extend Your Trip

You've already made the trip across an ocean, might as well make the most of it. Here's how we'd spend the extra time, before or after.

More itineraries to come

Before the Wedding: Rome & Beyond

Land Saturday, 10/9 (leave the Friday night before), then build your own path down to us. Here's a budget-friendly shape for it:

  • Days 1–2 or 3: Rome. Colosseum, Vatican, Trastevere at night, all the clichés are clichés for a reason. A hop-on-hop-off bus is a genuinely easy, cheap way to cover a lot of ground without planning every detail yourself.
  • Optional day trip: Florence. 2 hours by train from Rome, so it's doable even as a single day out and back if you don't want to add a full extra city to the trip.
  • End in Sorrento. Train down (see Getting There & Around), and settle into the coast before the wedding weekend starts.

If you'd rather add more than a day trip, here's what else is worth the detour from Rome:

  • Bologna (2 hrs 45 min) — Italy's food capital, less touristy, more real-college-town energy than postcard Italy. Go if you'd rather eat your way through a day than photograph it.
  • Salerno (2.5 hrs) — coastal, and conveniently right next door to where you're headed anyway, a smart pick if you don't want to backtrack.

Skip Pisa. It's one tower and a photo op, and we'd rather you spend that train time and hotel night somewhere that gives you more.

Whichever route you take, end in Sorrento. After Rome (and Florence or Bologna if you add them), it's the exhale, coastal, slower, the version of Italy you come back for.

After the Wedding: Stay on the Coast

Once the wedding's done, we'd stick around the Amalfi Coast rather than heading back north. It's a jarring comedown to trade the coast for Rome's pace right after, so we'd focus your extra days here instead:

  • Positano — the postcard town, cliffside, colorful, beautiful.
  • Amalfi — the town the whole coast is named for, and just as pretty.
  • Ravello — cliffside, historic, a little bit of a castle vibe if that's your thing.

If you'd rather go back to Rome for a few more days before flying home, that's completely fine too, just know it can feel abrasive after the slower coastal pace.

Weather tip: earlier in October tends to have nicer weather than later in the month, worth keeping in mind if you're deciding when to arrive or how long to stay.
