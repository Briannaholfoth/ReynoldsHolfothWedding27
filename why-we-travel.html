A Little Bit of Us

Why We Travel

If you know us, you know we love to travel. There are so many ways to see the world without draining your bank account! Exploring provides experiences that expand your comfort zone.

Travel isn't about escape. It's about growth/understanding and what it does to your head. Every trip hands you a slightly different version of what life could look like, different circumstances, different pace, different people who see the world in a way you've never considered. It's the fastest perspective shift we know.

"Travel is about the gorgeous feeling of teetering in the unknown." — Anthony Bourdain

That's the whole thing, really. You don't know the language. You don't know the currency. You don't fully know the customs, no matter how much you prepped. Every trip abroad is a small risk, and we've come to believe no risk means no reward.

The best parts haven't come from the trips that went perfectly either. They've come from the missed museum because a storm rolled in, the flight that got delayed, the plan that fell apart and turned into something better. That's where the actual growth happens, not the five-star stuff (though we don't mind that either).

And then there's the food. We're foodies, unapologetically. A big Italian meal with the people we love felt like the only way to do this.

"If I'm an advocate for anything, it's to move. As far as you can, as much as you can. Across the ocean, or simply across the river. The extent to which you can walk in someone else's shoes or at least eat their food, it's a plus for everybody. Open your mind, get up off the couch, move." — Anthony Bourdain

We hope this trip gives you a little bit of that same teetering, gorgeous unknown. Prego.
