A Little Bit of Us

Why We Love Travel

If you know us, you know we love to travel. There are so many ways to see the world without draining your bank account! Exploring provides experiences that expand your comfort zone.

Travel isn't about escape. It's about growth/understanding and what it does to your head and heart. Every trip hands you a slightly different version of what life could look like, different circumstances, different pace, different people who see the world in a way you've never considered. It's the fastest perspective shift we know.

"Travel is about the gorgeous feeling of teetering in the unknown." — Anthony Bourdain

That's the whole thing, really. You don't know the language. You don't know the currency. You don't fully know the customs, no matter how much you prepped. Every trip abroad is a small risk, but it pays the greatest dividends for the rest of your life.

The best parts haven't come from the trips that went perfectly either. They've come from the missed museum because a storm rolled in, the flight that got delayed, the plan that fell apart and turned into something better. It's not all always smooth sailing. That's where the actual growth happens, not the five-star stuff (though we don't mind that either).

Of course we want everything to go smoothly, and if it does that's great! If not, it usually makes for a heck of a story after and you got to practice meditation :)

We both LOVED Anthony Bourdain and try to bring his perspective with us when we're on the road. His advice, more or less, was to move: as far as you can, as much as you can, across an ocean or just across a river, walking in someone else's shoes and eating their food along the way. Get up off the couch and move. We couldn't agree more!

Lastly, we LOVE food. Great food. Great Coffee. Delicious libations. Travel diversifies the palate.

We hope this trip gives you a little bit of magic, romance, and excitement into the unknown. Prego!
